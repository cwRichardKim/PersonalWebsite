title: Tidy First?
---

When I focused on balancing creating options and changing behavior, what used to scare me now excited me: The more volatile the value of a potential behavior change, the better. The longer I could develop, the better. Yes, the cheaper I could develop in future, the better, but that was a small proportion of the value. The less design work I could do to create an option, the better.
