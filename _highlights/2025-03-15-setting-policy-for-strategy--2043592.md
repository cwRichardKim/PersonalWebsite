**Guidance** provides a recommendation about how a decision *should* be made. Guidance is useful when there’s enough nuance, [ambiguity](https://lethain.com/navigating-ambiguity/), or complexity that you *can* explain the desired destination, but you *can’t* mandate the path to reaching it.
