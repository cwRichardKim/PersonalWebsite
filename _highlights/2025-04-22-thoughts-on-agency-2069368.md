And yes, being able to outcompete others often requires agency. But competition is only a subset of agency, and not, in my experience, the most common or interesting one. Agency, more broadly understood, is about having (or requires) an accurate map of your interiority and a map of the external world, so you can navigate to situations where they overlap in generative ways.
