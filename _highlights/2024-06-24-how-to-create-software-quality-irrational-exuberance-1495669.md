I find it useful to recognize whether complexity is mostly driven by high scale (e.g. you’re performing 10,000s or 100,000s of requests per second) or whether complexity is mostly driven by a complex business domain (e.g. you’re trying to capture the intent of open-ended business contracts into a structured database). I think of the former as “scale complexity” and the later as “essential domain complexity”, extending the phrase from Fred Brooks’ [No Silver Bullet](https://lethain.us20.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f7003ed301623a88fab7cf783&id=d5aae5bf1e&e=c3050e6111).
